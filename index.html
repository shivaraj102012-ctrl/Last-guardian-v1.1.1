The Last Guardian

Chapter 1 — The Stolen Sunstone

Visit Elder Mira in Sunvale Village, explore Whispering Forest, and recover the Sunstone from the Thorn Warden.

Use arrow buttons to move, TALK near villagers, ATTACK near enemies, and POTION to heal.

Publish these files on GitHub Pages to install and enable offline play.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows • E talk • Space attack • H potion
